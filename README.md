HyperStar

Lead a star named Maia as high as she can go while dodging lasers.


Overview
--------

This adventure game follows a star named Maia as she attempts to go as high as possible. Along the way, lasers fire at different speeds, so you have to keep moving to stay alive.

Play it here: https://thecutebear486.itch.io/hyperstar


Features
--------

- Play as Maia, a star climbing as high as she can
- Dodge lasers that come out at different speeds
- Move with WASD or the arrow keys, whichever you prefer
